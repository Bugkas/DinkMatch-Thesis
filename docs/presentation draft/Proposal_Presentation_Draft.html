Situation Analysis: The Operational Problem

  • Current racket facilities rely on manual "paddle-rack" systems (First-In, First-Out queueing).
  • The Skill Disparity Dilemma: FIFO queueing matches players randomly by arrival, ignoring skill level, leading to unbalanced, low-quality matches.
  • Capacity Blindness: Manual systems cannot dynamically manage court assignments, leading to idle courts despite long player lines.
  • Social Friction: Physical queues encourage "ghost stacking" and "queue sniping" as players attempt to manipulate matchups.

Specific Objective 1 (Design Phase)

  • Objective Statement: Design and adapt a stateless, zero-sum point-margin-scaled Elo rating engine (PM-Elo) and a multi-objective matchmaking solver based on a parameterized cost function.
  • Why PM-Elo?
    • Calculates rating updates based on actual point share (ratio of points won) instead of a binary win/loss.
    • Operates statelessly to support local, offline-first kiosk functionality.
  • Matchmaking Solver: Dynamically selects the optimal group of 4 players from the queue based on a configurable cost function.

Presenter 4

Specific Objective 3.1 (Matchmaking Efficiency)

  • Objective Statement: Evaluate the matchmaking efficiency and queue dynamics of the DinkMatch system.
  • Methodology:
    • Discrete-event simulations built in Python using the SimPy library.
    • Synthetic player cohorts (N=50) modeled on Poisson arrival processes.
    • Two test branches: Control Group (Strict FIFO) vs. Experimental Group (DinkMatch Multi-Objective Solver).

SOO 3.1: Data Gathered & Analysis

  • Data Gathered (from 50 Simulation Runs):
    • Queue wait time / latency (seconds).

  • Data Analysis:
    • Independent Samples t-tests & ANOVA (at α = 0.05).
    • Tests for statistically significant differences in skill variance and wait times between the DinkMatch solver and the standard FIFO queue.

Specific Objective 3.2 (Rating Convergence)

  • Objective Statement: Evaluate the rating convergence and calibration accuracy compared to expert coordinator manual rankings.
  • Methodology:
    • Controlled empirical field trials at racket clubs in Agoo.
    • Cohort of twenty (n=20) walk-in players.
    • Resident facility organizers create a baseline "manual ranking" prior to system use.
    • Players use DinkMatch to play matches and automatically calibrate their PM-Elo ratings.
